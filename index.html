A Letter From The Heart
Dear Mom,

There are many people in this world, but no one could ever take your place in my heart.
You are the reason I learned love, kindness, sacrifice, and strength.

Every smile of mine carries your prayers behind it.
Every success of mine carries your sacrifices behind it.

You stayed awake when I slept peacefully.
You worried when I smiled carelessly.
You gave me strength even when you were tired yourself.

I may not always express my feelings perfectly, but today I want you to know this from the deepest part of my heart:

You are the greatest blessing of my life.

No matter how much I grow, no matter where life takes me, I will always remain the child whose safest place is his mother’s love.

Thank you for every silent sacrifice, every prayer, every tear, every smile, and every moment you gave to make my life better.

If I become a good human someday, it will only be because I was raised by a mother like you.

Happy Mother’s Day, Mom.
You are my home, my strength, my peace, and my forever inspiration.

I love you endlessly ❤️
— Your Son, Neeraj Dwivedi
